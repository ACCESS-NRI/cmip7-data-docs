# Citing requirements and errata

## Citing and terms of use

To enable modelling groups and others who support CMIP7 to demonstrate its impact (and secure ongoing funding), you are required to cite and acknowledge those who have made CMIP7 possible. The mandatory requirements below are copied from the [CMIP7 Guidance for users](https://wcrp-cmip.github.io/cmip7-guidance/docs/CMIP7/Guidance_for_users/#2-terms-of-use-and-citations-requirements), which also lists recommended best practices in addition to the mandatory steps below.

!!! tip "Check the CMIP7 guidance for the latest requirements"
    CMIP7 citation requirements are still being finalised, and the [CMIP7 Guidance for users](https://wcrp-cmip.github.io/cmip7-guidance/docs/CMIP7/Guidance_for_users/#2-terms-of-use-and-citations-requirements) always has the most up-to-date information. Before you submit a paper or report that uses CMIP7 data, it's best to double-check the requirements there.

??? info "1. Cite the specific dataset(s) used"
    For each model whose data is used, please include:

    - A citation in the form of:

        > Authors/Data Creators (publication year): Title. Earth System Grid Federation. DOI.

        e.g.

        > Swart et al. (2019). CCCma CanESM5 model output prepared for CMIP6 CMIP historical. Earth System Grid Federation. https://doi.org/10.22033/ESGF/CMIP6.3610 .

    - A table with at minimum the models ("sources"), institutions, and data citations as above. Adding the dataset version in the table is also strongly recommended. If the journal has a citation limit, putting the table in the Supporting Information is acceptable.
    - A data availability statement pointing to the table and acknowledging ESGF is required.

    ??? question "How to find the DOI and the version?"

        The first CMIP7 DOIs are just being minted, so information will be added here as it becomes available. See more details in the [CMIP7 Guidance for users](https://wcrp-cmip.github.io/cmip7-guidance/docs/CMIP7/Guidance_for_users/#2-terms-of-use-and-citations-requirements). 

??? info "2. Acknowledge CMIP7"
    In the Acknowledgment section, insert the following text:

    > We acknowledge the World Climate Research Programme's Coupled Model Intercomparison Project contributors who coordinated and promoted CMIP7. We thank the climate modelling groups for producing and making available their model output, the Earth System Grid Federation (ESGF) for archiving the data and providing access, and the multiple funding agencies who support CMIP7 and ESGF.

??? info "3. Adhere to the license"
    Adhere to the license conditions listed in the global attribute of each dataset.

## Errata

??? info "Find and report CMIP7 errata"
    CMIP errata are now managed via the [ESGF Errata Service](https://errata.esgf.io/). This includes the functionality to:

    - [search CMIP errata](https://errata.esgf.io/static/index.html) by project, experiment, or variable; and
    - report [suspected issues/errata](https://errata.esgf.io/static/edit.html).

    Issues should be reported to ESGF, which will notify the modelling group. The modelling group will review the issue and determine the appropriate resolution. NCI will be notified if the issue is confirmed and action is required. If NCI-published CMIP data is retracted and replaced, the new version will be published.

    A link to the NCI errata page for CMIP7 will be shared here when available.
