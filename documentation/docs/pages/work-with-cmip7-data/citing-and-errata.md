# Citing requirements and errata

## Citing and terms of use

To enable modelling groups and others who support CMIP7 to demonstrate its impact (and secure ongoing funding), you are required to cite and acknowledge those who have made CMIP7 possible. The mandatory requirements below are copied from the [CMIP7 Guidance for users](https://wcrp-cmip.github.io/cmip7-guidance/docs/CMIP7/Guidance_for_users/#2-terms-of-use-and-citations-requirements), which also lists recommended best practices in addition to the mandatory steps below.


!!! warning "CMIP7 citation details still being finalized"
    Some of the CMIP7 citation requirements and guidance from the global community are still being finalized. We include some information below, and will add more details as they become available.

### Mandatory steps for citing CMIP7-related work

??? info "1. Cite the specific dataset(s) used"
    For each model whose data is used, please include:

 
    1. **A citation.** *Note: the exact information to include in the citation is still being finalized by the global CMIP community. There are currently 2 suggested citation formats and we will follow up here once that is agreed upon. Please check back here soon for updated information.*
    2. **A table** with at minimum the models ("sources"), institutions, and data citations as above. Adding the dataset version in the table is also strongly recommended. If the journal has a citation limit, putting the table in the Supporting Information is acceptable.
    3. **A data availability statement** pointing to the table and acknowledging ESGF.

    ??? question "How to find the DOI and the version?"

        You can view published records (including DOI) in the [CMIP7 citation service](https://cmip7-citations.ceda.ac.uk/citations/?search=&published_only=yes&project_id=all&activity_id=all&institution_id=all&source_id=all).

        *Note that the first CMIP7 DOIs are just being minted, so more information will be added here as it becomes available.*
        
        See more details in the [CMIP7 Guidance for users](https://wcrp-cmip.github.io/cmip7-guidance/docs/CMIP7/Guidance_for_users/#2-terms-of-use-and-citations-requirements). 

??? info "2. Acknowledge CMIP7, NCI, and ACCESS-NRI"
    
    In the Acknowledgment section, please include the following text depending on the resources you have used.

    1. **Acknowledge CMIP7**

        > We acknowledge the World Climate Research Programme's Coupled Model Intercomparison Project contributors who coordinated and promoted CMIP7. We thank the climate modelling groups for producing and making available their model output, the Earth System Grid Federation (ESGF) for archiving the data and providing access, and the multiple funding agencies who support CMIP7 and ESGF.

    2. **Acknowledge NCI** if you used any NCI resources, such as compute, storage, documentation (like this site), or other support. [NCI acknowledgement statement](https://nci.org.au/users/nci-terms-and-conditions-access#Reporting)

    3. **Acknowledge ACCESS-NRI** if you used any ACCESS-NRI models, data, tools, workflows, documentation (like this site), or other support. [ACCESS-NRI acknowledgement statement](https://www.access-nri.org.au/resources/acknowledging-us/)

??? info "3. Adhere to the license"
    Adhere to the license conditions listed in the global attribute of each dataset.

## Errata

??? info "Find and report CMIP7 errata"
    CMIP errata are now managed via the [ESGF Errata Service](https://errata.esgf.io/). This includes the functionality to:

    - [search CMIP errata](https://errata.esgf.io/static/index.html) by project, experiment, or variable; and
    - report [suspected issues/errata](https://errata.esgf.io/static/edit.html).

    Issues should be reported to ESGF, which will notify the modelling group. The modelling group will review the issue and determine the appropriate resolution. NCI will be notified if the issue is confirmed and action is required. If NCI-published CMIP data is retracted and replaced, the new version will be published.

    A link to the NCI errata page for CMIP7 will be shared here when available.
