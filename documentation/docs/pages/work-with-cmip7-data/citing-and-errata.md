# Citing requirements and errata

## Citing and terms of use

??? info "How to cite CMIP7 data"
    Citation and terms of use of CMIP data is very important to enable modelling groups and funders to track and demonstrate impact. You are required to cite and acknowledge all CMIP data. All data published through ESGF will have a DOI created and a preferred citation. For each model whose data is used, please include a citation in the form of:

    > Authors/Data Creators (publication year): Title. Science and Technology Facilities Council. DOI.

    Detailed guidance can be found at [CMIP7 Guidance for users](https://wcrp-cmip.github.io/cmip7-guidance/docs/CMIP7/Guidance_for_users/).

## Errata

??? info "Find and report CMIP7 errata"
    CMIP errata are now managed via the [ESGF Errata Service](https://errata.esgf.io/). This includes the functionality to:

    - [search CMIP errata](https://errata.esgf.io/static/index.html) by project, experiment, or variable; and
    - report [suspected issues/errata](https://errata.esgf.io/static/edit.html).

    Issues should be reported to ESGF, which will notify the modelling group. The modelling group will review the issue and determine the appropriate resolution. NCI will be notified if the issue is confirmed and action is required. If NCI-published CMIP data is retracted and replaced, the new version will be published.

    Project members will receive email announcements regarding relevant errata when they occur.

    Data held by NCI will have errata notifications on the [Errata and Known Issues](https://opus.nci.org.au/spaces/CMIP/pages/288227530/Errata+and+Known+Issues+for+CORDEX-CMIP6+and+Regional+Downscaling) page.
