# Data currently available and what's coming

## What's available around the world and at NCI?

CMIP7 data is being published by modelling centres around the world, and much of it is also available locally on NCI's Gadi supercomputer.

??? info "CMIP7 data published around the world"

    The [Climate resource dashboard](https://www.climate-resource.com/tools/esm-model/cmip7-availability) gives an overview of CMIP7 data publication progress: it tracks how much of the requested data each modelling centre has published to ESGF so far, by model and experiment. It's a status summary, not a tool for searching or downloading data.

??? info "CMIP7 data available on NCI"

    The best way to see what CMIP7 data is available locally on NCI is the [ACCESS-NRI Interactive Data Catalogue](https://access-nri.github.io/interactive-data-catalogue/#/), which lets you browse and search the datasets held on Gadi. You can access CMIP7 data directly with one of the quick links at the top of the webpage.

## What's coming next?

??? info "ACCESS model simulations"

    You can follow which ACCESS models are currently being run for CMIP7 and which simulations are planned for later on the [ACCESS CMIP7 dashboard](https://access-nri.github.io/cmip7-dashboard/).

??? info "International data replication"

    All monthly CMIP7 data is currently being replicated to NCI. Broader replication priorities are still being decided, and we will consult with the research community before they are finalised. We are not taking requests for specific datasets at this time; more details on this will be shared soon.
