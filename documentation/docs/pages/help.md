## Asking on the ACCESS-Hive Forum

### How it works

??? info "How to ask for help on the Forum"
    1. Have a quick search of the Forum. Your question may already have an answer!
    2. [Create a Forum account](https://forum.access-hive.org.au/docs?topic=884) if you don't have one yet. (You only need to do this once.)
    3. Click the [Ask on the ACCESS-Hive Forum](#need-help) button at the top of this page to start your post. It's already set up in the CMIP7 category with the **help** tag and includes a short template to guide you.
    4. ACCESS-NRI Support or someone from the community will reply to help you out.

### Private and anonymous options
