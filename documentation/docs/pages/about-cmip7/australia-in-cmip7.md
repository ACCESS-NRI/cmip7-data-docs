# Australia's role in CMIP7

## ACCESS Earth System Model (ACCESS-ESM)

An Earth System Model (ESM) simulates the carbon and other bio-chemical cycles alongside climate. The ACCESS Earth System Model (ACCESS-ESM) is a fully-coupled global climate model that includes atmosphere, land, ocean, sea ice, ocean biogeochemistry and land biogeochemistry components, linked together by a coupler.

Read more about ACCESS-ESM on the [ACCESS-NRI website](https://www.access-nri.org.au/models/earth-system-model-esm/).

## ACCESS Consortium

The ACCESS Consortium brings together Australian institutions contributing to the Australian Community Climate and Earth System Simulator (ACCESS) and its submissions to the Coupled Model Intercomparison Project (CMIP). Participating organisations contribute to different ACCESS models and scientific activities, supported through a combination of institutional investment and Australian Government research infrastructure and research programs, together with international collaborations.

## ACCESS and the CMIP7 Assessment Fast Track

??? info "Australia's model: ACCESS-ESM1.6"
    ACCESS-ESM1.6 is an Earth system model developed for Australia's contribution to the CMIP7 Assessment Fast Track. [CSIRO](https://www.csiro.au/en/) led the development in collaboration with other members of the [ACCESS Consortium](#access-consortium). This version includes several improvements over ACCESS-ESM1.5 used for CMIP6. Simulation of Southern Ocean circulation, sea ice, and heat uptake, which play an important role in shaping Australia's climate, have been improved by a pseudo-iceberg scheme that models how meltwater enters and is transported through the ocean. The new model introduces distinct eucalypt- and acacia-dominated vegetation types, improving simulations of water use, carbon uptake, and ecosystem responses to drought and climate change. It also includes improved land-use change processes, more realistically representing Australia's contribution to the global carbon cycle.

    - [Essential Model Documentation (EMD)](https://wcrp-cmip.github.io/Essential-Model-Documentation/docs/Model/?model=access-esm1-6)
    - [Model Configurations](https://github.com/ACCESS-NRI/access-esm1.6-configs#access-esm16-model-configurations)

??? info "Why ACCESS-ESM1.6 is well suited to CMIP7"
    The new model is well positioned to deliver to CMIP7, which has an increased focus on experiments driven by CO~2~ *emissions* rather than prescribed CO~2~ *concentrations*. The inclusion of the carbon-cycle in the land and ocean codes directly models processes that transport atmospheric carbon between these systems, providing a more realistic assessment of future climate for projected policy impacts such as emissions reduction pathways and net-zero targets.
