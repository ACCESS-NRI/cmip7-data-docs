# CMIP7 Data in Australia

<!-- Working title — update once a final page title is settled. -->

The Coupled Model Intercomparison Project Phase 7 (CMIP7) is an international climate modelling initiative that brings together simulations from modelling centres around the world to support climate research, projections and assessment.

In Australia, NCI will provide access to Australian CMIP7 outputs and a growing collection of international CMIP7 data. This work includes supporting the publication of Australian model outputs and providing access to international data as it becomes available.

## Funding and partners

As part of the 2025 NCRIS funding round, ACCESS-NRI and the National Computational Infrastructure (NCI) have secured new support for CMIP7 data publication and access in Australia. The funding also supports the upgrade of the Earth System Grid Federation (ESGF) node hosted at NCI, which supports the publication and sharing of climate model data with the international research community.

This work forms part of the broader **Australia's Next-Generation Earth System Model** activity, led by ACCESS-NRI in partnership with NCI, the Terrestrial Ecosystem Research Network (TERN), the Commonwealth Scientific and Industrial Research Organisation (CSIRO) and the University of New South Wales (UNSW). The new funding supports the development of Australia's next generation of climate and Earth system modelling capability, alongside the infrastructure needed to produce, publish and use the data outputs.

## What will be supported

The new funding will support Australia's participation in the Coupled Model Intercomparison Project Phase 7 (CMIP7), including publication of Australian model outputs and access to international CMIP7 data through NCI.

The full scope and priorities for the Australian CMIP7 data collection are still being developed, with further details to be added to these pages as they are finalised.
